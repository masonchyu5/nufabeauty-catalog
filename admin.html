2 · Product images

Drop individual photos or a whole folder. These become the master copies the catalog is built from — originals are kept as-is (photos over 4 MB are shrunk just enough to fit the upload limit). A file with the same name as one already in the catalog replaces it; new names are added and used once the CSV refers to them. Accepted: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp. Uploads start immediately and resume if the page reloads.

3 · Delete photos

Every photo in the catalog. A product shows the photo whose filename matches its SKU. “in catalog” is being shown; “not shown” means a photo with a higher-priority extension wins for that SKU (jpg › webp › png › jpeg); “no product” means no product has that SKU. The last two are dead weight and safe to delete. Selecting a photo removes it — and its display copy — when you publish.
